# Conference Guidelines (ICLR / ICML style draft)

- **Page limit:** 9 pages of main content (excluding references and appendix), targeting ~8–10 pages total for the draft.
- **Format:** Single-column `article` class acceptable for draft; two-column conference style preferred for final submission.
- **Mandatory sections:** Abstract, Introduction, Related Work, Method/Problem Formulation, Experiments/Results, Conclusion. Limitations encouraged.
- **Anonymity:** Double-blind. Anonymous authors only. No acknowledgements revealing identity.
- **Citations:** Numeric or author-year fine; use BibTeX. Prefer peer-reviewed venues.
- **Figures/Tables:** Captions must be self-contained. Do not fabricate numeric results. Mark unavailable experiments honestly or omit them.
- **Cutoff date for literature:** 2026-06-01
- **Submission deadline (reference):** 2026-09-30
